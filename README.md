# 🎨 Problem Statement 1 – Skin Color Analysis for Optimal Fashion

## Problem

Gen Z consumers want to dress well and follow fashion trends, but choosing clothing colors that complement their skin tone can be challenging.

Selecting an unsuitable color may affect the overall appearance of an outfit. Therefore, we proposed a personalized fashion recommendation solution based on the user's skin tone.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

We designed a system that analyzes the user's skin tone and recommends suitable clothing colors.

The proposed process includes:

1. Gathering information about the user's skin tone through questions or skin scanning.
2. Using machine learning to classify the skin tone into categories such as warm, cool, and neutral.
3. Matching the identified skin tone with suitable clothing colors.
4. Providing personalized clothing color recommendations
   
## 🎯 Goal

To help users make better fashion choices and confidently select clothing colors that complement their skin tone.

## ✨ Benefits

- Helps users make informed clothing choices
- Enhances personal appearance and confidence
- Provides a more personalized approach to fashion styling
- Makes fashion recommendations more relevant

  # 👗 Problem Statement 2 – Myntra Rental Store
  
## Problem

Designer dresses and luxury accessories can be expensive and are often purchased for special occasions only.

Since users may not want to repeatedly wear the same outfit at different events, expensive fashion items can remain underused, resulting in unnecessary expenditure and resource wastage.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

We proposed a **Rental Store feature within the Myntra app** that allows users to rent premium dresses and accessories for a specific period instead of purchasing them.

Users can:

- Browse premium and designer items
- Select an item for a specific rental period
- Rent the item through the Myntra app
- Choose delivery options
- Return the item after use
- Get insurance coverage for potential damages
  ## 🎯 Goal

To make premium and occasion-based fashion more accessible while encouraging sustainable fashion consumption.

## ✨ Benefits

- Access to trendy outfits for special occasions
- Enjoy high-end fashion at a fraction of the purchase cost
- Reduces fashion waste
- Encourages sustainable consumption
- Provides users with more fashion choices

 # 2. Rental Store – UI/UX Design 👗

A modern Rental Store UI/UX design created to provide users with a simple and convenient way to browse and rent outfits for special occasions.

The design focuses on making the rental experience visually appealing and easy to navigate, while clearly presenting product details, original prices, rental prices, and rental durations.

## 🎯 Objective

To design a user-friendly rental shopping platform where customers can discover stylish outfits and rent them for a specific period instead of purchasing them.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 Search for brands and products
- 👗 Browse different fashion categories
- ✨ Explore exclusive wear
- 🛍️ View top rental products
- 💰 Compare original and rental prices
- 📅 Check rental availability and duration
- 👤 User profile section
- 🏠 Simple and intuitive navigation

## 🎨 Design Focus

The UI was designed with a clean and fashion-focused visual style, emphasizing:

- Easy product discovery
- Clear price comparison
- Simple navigation
- Product-focused layouts
- Convenient rental information
